# Competition TV artwork

Owner-supplied PNGs imported 26 September 2026. All templates are 1920 × 1080
(16:9); sources 3–6 were reduced from 8000 × 4500. Originals remain untouched.
No text, logos or design elements were added to the source artwork.

`CompetitionSlideTemplates` replaces the outlined guide with a QR including a
four-module white quiet zone. Coordinates are pixels on the normalized canvas:

| Template | QR x | QR y | Square size |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 124 | 104 | 663 |
| 2 | 1142 | 121 | 663 |
| 3–6 | 1141 | 120 | 664 |

The one-pixel expansion removes the guide’s antialiased outline. Everything
outside this region is preserved exactly in the generated lossless PNG. Event
and DancePro logos are separate player overlays, not part of these files.
