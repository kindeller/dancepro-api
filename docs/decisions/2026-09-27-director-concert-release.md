# Director review and concert video release

Status: accepted through the requested tracker workflow, 27 September 2026.

Concert editing and upload completion do not grant family access. Studio owners
and directors review the latest verified upload batch through authenticated
Studio Hub routes. The same review record supplies corrections to the tracker.
Approval and final release are one director action, publishing only the batch
that was reviewed. Family access still requires the existing video-code flow.

Each feedback submission advances a review revision and resets the correction
confirmations. Correction emails carry the revision and batch they describe.
They cannot complete a newer review, and stale queued messages are cancelled.

Photo publication and its announcement are separate actions. Announcements use
existing tracked email delivery and local rehearsal transport, with a unique
release-group/recipient pair to avoid repeated sends. No external email is
sent merely by opening or releasing a gallery.
