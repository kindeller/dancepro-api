# Concert tracker task guide

Updated 27 September 2026. The same instructions are available under **Task instructions** on each task page.

Use **Back to tracker** and **Back to event** in the top-left of the tracker bar. The first preserves your tracker view; the second opens the scheduling event. Booking-wide tasks use the first active event; pending bookings link to their event details until scheduling exists. Crew-only editing retains its authorised My editing navigation.

The tracker bar changes responsibility, deadlines and follow-up notes. Automatic statuses follow their source records. Clearing a custom deadline restores the standard task deadline. Yellow means due within three days; red means overdue or a specifically flagged action. Photo uploads have their own waiting/uploading rules. Grey means not required.

Emails marked **Test** are captured locally and are not delivered to real recipients. Queued or scheduled is not sent. Read delivery history before using the protected resend action.

## 1.1 Booking & services

1. Review the studio contact, services, event dates, venues and requirements. Save any changes before approval.
2. Resolve proposed venues, then use Approve booking (or Approved in the status box). Review and send the confirmation email on the next page.
3. Booking approval completes 1.1. Confirmation delivery is recorded separately; approval alone does not send an email.

## 1.2 Seats & families email

1. Review the recipient and email preview, including the link to the Studio Hub.
2. Send the email once. A successful send completes 1.2 and sets the 1.3 deadline to 10 days after sending.
3. If no email is needed, choose Approved in the status box. This does not send a message or start the email-based deadline. Check delivery history before resending.

## 1.3 Seats & families numbers

1. Count each family once across the booking, including performing siblings and parents. Quoted numbers are estimates until explicitly confirmed.
2. Confirm the final family total, then open each concert and save the required crew seat allocations.
3. Completion is automatic only when families are confirmed and seats are recorded for every concert. Each update records the staff member and date. DR portraits are not required.

## 1.4 Concert week email

1. Check the event details and final requirements in the preview; the standard deadline is seven days before this event.
2. Send once and check the delivery result. Successful delivery completes this task; queued or scheduled messages are not yet complete.
3. Use Approved only if the email is unnecessary. An additional copy requires explicit resend confirmation.

## 2.1 Crew ready

1. Use the embedded event availability sheet to assign the required roles for this event.
2. Publish the roster and obtain the assigned crew acknowledgements. Resolve the outstanding availability requirements shown on the sheet.
3. Readiness follows the same roster as full Event availability. The tracker assignee identifies task responsibility and does not assign a crew role.

## 2.2 Prepare programme

1. Use the studio-uploaded programme or upload/replace it as admin.
2. Read the programme, check the extracted items, spelling and running order, then save and confirm the working programme.
3. Review any changes before confirming again. Uploader work and video titles must use the current confirmed programme.

## 2.3 Video titles

1. Confirm the programme first, then review the item titles, subtitles, logo, colours and title-card previews.
2. Use Video titles ready to publish the JPEG package and make it downloadable in the uploader. Saving the template or downloading a preview alone does not complete the task.
3. If the programme or title content changes, review and publish a fresh package. Reopening the task withdraws the ready download.

## 2.4 Post-concert email

1. Review the post-concert message and studio recipient. The standard deadline is the day after this event.
2. Send once and check delivery history. Successful delivery completes the task.
3. Choose Approved if the email is unnecessary; review the previous delivery before confirming any resend.

## 2.5 Studio videos & slideshows

1. Select the concert and review every current studio video, opening clip and slideshow. Admin can upload or replace files received separately.
2. Approve each usable file or request a replacement. All current files must be approved for automatic completion.
3. If files are held outside the portal, record receipt manually with a note. Choose Not required when no studio media is needed. Confirm their inclusion in the final exports in 3.4.

## 3.1 Photo uploads

1. Check the current working programme and each photographer role. Queued and verified counts come from the laptop uploader.
2. Each required photographer must finish their queue and confirm Finished upload in the uploader. All required roles must be complete.
3. A waiver records missing work but does not itself complete an upload or release the gallery. Waiting stays blue until overdue; uploading is blue and complete is green.

## 3.2 Concert editing

1. Assign the editor in the tracker bar. Download the available programme, published titles and studio videos below.
2. Edit the full concert consistently, in programme order, including all required titles and supplied studio media.
3. Mark the status Complete when the edit is ready for the final quality check. This does not verify uploads, approve the concert or release it to customers.

## 3.3 Final quality check

1. Review the full edit against all 20 checks for files, titles, video and audio.
2. Tick checks as you go; each change saves automatically. Watch for the saved confirmation or any error before leaving.
3. All checks are required. Complete all is available only to admin accounts. Unchecking a check reopens the task.

## 3.4 Video uploads

1. Register and upload the final concert video queue from the laptop uploader. Wait for every file to be verified and confirm Finished Uploading.
2. Complete editing and the quality checklist. Approve the current studio videos/slideshows and record which verified export includes each one.
3. Resolve every readiness reason shown here. The standard deadline is 10 days after this event; the task completes automatically when the current concert is ready for review.

## 3.5 Video approval link

1. Finish the current video uploads, editing, quality checks and studio-media inclusions first. Missing requirements appear above the email action.
2. Review the director recipient and private approval link, then send once. Successful delivery completes this email task.
3. The director reviews through their account. Sending the link does not release the video. Check delivery history before resending.

## 3.6 Director corrections

1. Read the director feedback and make the requested changes. Confirm Corrections complete when the edit is finished.
2. Upload and verify the corrected concert, complete its readiness checks, then confirm Corrections uploaded.
3. Both checks open the corrections email. Send it to complete the task. New feedback reopens the task; release without any feedback shows No corrections.

## 4.1 Photo release

1. Finish every required photographer upload and prepare the studio photo albums and release group. The target is 48 hours after the final booking event.
2. Resolve the release reasons shown for each group, choose its cover and watermark, then select Release all albums.
3. Release publishes the gallery and opens an announcement for registered studio accounts. Review and send it separately; gallery release alone does not email customers.

## 4.2 Invoice & payment

1. Approve and link the booking to its studio, and confirm final family numbers before preparing the video invoice.
2. Review the draft, prices, discounts and notes. Send invoice saves it to the Studio Hub and opens the invoice email; send that email to start the 14-day payment deadline.
3. Before sending, the standard deadline is seven days before the final event. Afterwards the task stays Not paid until settlement; review payment details or record a received bank transfer. Download the PDF from the invoice page.

## 4.3 Final video release

1. Ensure the current video is ready for studio review and any requested corrections have been completed, uploaded and emailed.
2. The studio owner/director uses Release concert video on their review page. This automatically approves and releases the current version.
3. The studio then shares its video Access Code with families. Admin does not manually complete this task; the tracker follows the director release.
